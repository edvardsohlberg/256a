Three visualizer ideas

  1. I would love to create a visualizer similar to the lightbulb example in lecture. I have a soundsystem and TV in my room, so being able to plug the aux directly into my computer and allow listeners to customize the visualizer's behavior with a HUD in real time would be awesome.

  2. Related to the last idea, but as we continue to listen to songs via the visualizer, they will be added to a kind of song bank in the form of a nighttime city. As we navigate the city, we can place songs in certain locations, and have them play ambiently as we move around and get close to each place.

  3. As its own independent idea, I would like to draw a spectrum at the bottom of the screen, but represented as a row of piano keys. As sounds play, the keys highlight as if pressed, and a colored streak shoots up from each like a piano visualizer in reverse. I could even play the dorm piano into it to test and align the correct frequencies with the correct notes.
